The day before the wedding we will stay in Wiktorowo.
If you want to join us earlier we will organise an aperitivo on Friday.

To get there you can take a flight to either Poznan, Wroclaw, or Gdansk.
The drive from Poznan airport is around one hour and a half.
If needed or there is demand we can organise a shuttle from Poznan airport to the venue or pick you up at the airport
Look here for a list of accomdation near Wiktorowo.


Renting a car would be the easiest way to the venue, also if you want to explore the area. There is plenty of parking available at the venue. We've personally used had good experiences renting from Zest Car Rental


If you prefer to take a train, you can take the train to Inowroclaw, which is the nearest town to the venue. The train station is a 35 minute drive from the venue.
Look here for a list of trains to Inowroclaw.


If you prefer to take a bus, you can take the bus to Znin, which is the nearest town to the venue. The bus station is a 10 minute drive from the venue.
Look here for a list of buses to Znin.
